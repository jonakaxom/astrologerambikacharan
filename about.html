About Ambika Charan

Ambika Charan has practised Vedic astrology since 1975 from Fulguri, Panigaon, in Nalbari district, Assam. Over nearly fifty years, people have come to him with questions about marriage, work, children, health and the right time to begin something new.

His approach is simple: read the chart carefully, explain it in plain words and suggest remedies that are practical. He accepts only dakshina, so cost never stands between a family and guidance.

Astrology offers perspective and does not guarantee outcomes. Please also seek proper medical, legal and financial advice where needed.
