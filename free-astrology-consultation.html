Free astrology consultation: questions

Is the astrology consultation really free?

Ambika Charan does not charge a fixed fee for any consultation. You may offer dakshina if you wish, and the amount is entirely your choice.

What does dakshina mean?

Dakshina is a voluntary offering made with respect, in the Indian tradition of thanking a teacher or guide. There is no rate, and no minimum.

Is online or phone consultation available?

No. Only offline, in-person consultation is available at Fulguri, Panigaon, Nalbari district. The phone is used only to fix a time.

Can people from other states and cities in India visit?

Yes. People from anywhere in India are welcome. Since consultation is in person only, please call 7086234534 to fix a time before you travel.

What should I bring for a free kundali reading?

Your date, exact time and place of birth, an old horoscope if you have one, and your questions written down. For marriage matching bring the details of both people.

What can I ask about?

Janam kundali, marriage matching, career, education, health concerns, doshas, gemstones, muhurat and Vastu.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call first so a time can be fixed. Walk-ins may not find him free.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Astrology gives guidance and perspective, not guaranteed outcomes.

Astrology offers guidance and perspective and does not guarantee results. Please also take medical, legal and financial advice where needed.
