Questions about dakshina

What is dakshina?

Dakshina is a voluntary offering made with respect, in the Indian tradition of honouring a teacher or guide. You give what you feel is right and what you can afford.

Is there a minimum amount?

No. There is no fixed price for any service. No one is turned away for offering little.

What do I need for a birth chart reading?

Your date, exact time and place of birth. If you do not know the time, a Prashna chart or palm reading can still help.
